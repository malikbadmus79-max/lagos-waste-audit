# Resident Survey: Household Waste Collection in Lagos

Questionnaire version 2. Language: English. Mode: Google Forms, distributed by link. Estimated completion time: 5 minutes. The form is generated by `survey/build_form.gs`.

## Consent text

This short survey is part of an independent study of household waste collection in Lagos. It takes about 5 minutes. No names, phone numbers or house addresses are collected. Answers are reported only in summary form. Taking part is voluntary.

0. I am 18 or older and agree to take part in this survey. (Yes / No; "No" ends the form)

## Section A: Location

1. Which Local Government Area (LGA) do you live in? (Agege, Ajeromi-Ifelodun, Alimosho, Amuwo-Odofin, Apapa, Badagry, Epe, Eti-Osa, Ibeju-Lekki, Ifako-Ijaiye, Ikeja, Ikorodu, Kosofe, Lagos Island, Lagos Mainland, Mushin, Ojo, Oshodi-Isolo, Shomolu, Surulere, Not sure)
2. Which area or neighbourhood do you live in? (Short text; area name only)
3. Which best describes the road in front of your home? (Tarred, good condition / Tarred but badly damaged / Untarred (sand or laterite) / Footpath only)
4. Can a refuse truck drive up to your gate? (Yes / No / Not sure)

## Section B: Home

5. What type of home do you live in? (Room in a shared house (face-me-I-face-you) / Self-contained or mini flat / Flat with 2 or more bedrooms / Duplex or detached house / Other)
6. Is your home inside a gated estate or close with a residents' association? (Yes / No)

## Section C: Collection service

7. Who mainly collects your household waste? (PSP or LAWMA truck / Cart pusher / Estate or landlord arranges it / Household burns or buries it / Household dumps it (drain, roadside, open plot) / Don't know)
8. How often is the PSP or LAWMA truck supposed to collect your waste? (Weekly / Every two weeks / Monthly / No fixed schedule / Don't know / Not applicable, we don't use the truck)
9. In the last 4 weeks, how many times did the PSP or LAWMA truck actually collect your waste? (0 / 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 or more / Don't know / Not applicable, we don't use the truck)
10. When was your waste last collected (by anyone)? (Within the last 7 days / 8 to 14 days ago / 15 to 30 days ago / More than a month ago / Never collected / Can't remember)
11. When the truck does not come, what does your household usually do? (Multiple answers: Wait for the next visit / Call the PSP or LAWMA / Pay a cart pusher / Burn it / Dump it / Take it to a collection point / Other)

## Section D: Payment

12. Does your household pay a waste bill? (Yes, regularly / Yes, sometimes / No / Landlord or estate pays (included in rent or service charge) / Don't know)
13. Roughly how much does your household pay per month? (Nothing / Under ₦1,000 / ₦1,000 to ₦2,999 / ₦3,000 to ₦4,999 / ₦5,000 to ₦9,999 / ₦10,000 or more / Landlord or estate pays / Don't know)
14. If your household does not pay, or pays only sometimes, what are the main reasons? (Multiple answers: Not applicable, we pay regularly / Service is poor or irregular / Cannot afford it / Never received a bill / Don't know who to pay / Pay a cart pusher instead / Other)

## Section E: Overall

15. Overall, how satisfied are you with waste collection where you live? (1 = Very dissatisfied to 5 = Very satisfied)
16. Is there anything else you would like to say about waste collection in your area? (Optional long text)

## Design notes

- Q2 allows responses with "Not sure" in Q1 to be assigned to an LGA during cleaning.
- Q8 and Q9 refer to the PSP or LAWMA truck and include a "Not applicable" option, so households served by cart pushers are not counted as missed truck collections.
- Q10 uses time bands instead of a calendar date. Band midpoints used to estimate days since last collection are recorded in `docs/assumptions.md`.
- Google sign-in is not required, because sign-in reduces completion rates for links shared by messaging apps. Duplicate submissions are identified during cleaning (identical answers submitted within a short interval).
- Interview participants are recruited separately, so the form collects no contact details.
- Version 1 was revised before piloting: truck-specific wording and "Not applicable" options in Q8 and Q9, time bands in Q10, a "Landlord or estate pays" option in Q13, a "Not applicable" option in Q14, and the local housing term in Q5.
