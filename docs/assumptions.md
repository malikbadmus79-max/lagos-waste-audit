# Assumptions

Log of assumptions made in the analysis, referenced by ID wherever they are used in code, notebooks or reports.

| ID | Assumption | Reason | Used in | Sensitivity |
|----|------------|--------|---------|-------------|
| A01 | Monthly and weekly totals are converted to daily averages by dividing by the number of calendar days in the period, counting both end dates (31 days for May 2026; 7 days for 28 July to 3 August 2026). | Official statements do not say whether weekends and public holidays are operating days. | `lagos_waste.official_stats.daily_average`; notebook 01 | Using 26 operating days for May 2026 would raise the daily average from 13,500 to 16,096 t/day, widening the gap with stated PSP capacity. |
| A02 | Truck trips to disposal facilities carry 10 tonnes each on average. | In S02, both tonnage figures equal exactly 10 tonnes per trip (18,660 / 1,866 and 3,490 / 349), which indicates that official tonnage is derived from trip counts at this rate. | Conversion of the 2021 trip counts in S07; notebook 01 | At 8 or 12 tonnes per trip, 850 daily trips in 2021 would equal 6,800 or 10,200 t/day instead of 8,500 t/day. |
| A03 | Point-in-time statements (operator counts, fleet sizes, generation estimates) refer to the publication date of the source. | Sources rarely state the reference date of a count. | `data/processed/official_stats.csv` (period_start and period_end) | Shifts dates by weeks, not years; no effect on the comparisons in notebook 01. |
| A04 | Daily waste generation is 13,000 to 15,000 t/day throughout 2021 to 2026. | This is the most frequently stated official range [S03, S06, S09]; no source reports a measured series. | Ratios of evacuation to generation in notebook 01 | At the 16,000 t/day upper value in S08, the May 2026 evacuation figure would equal 84% of generation instead of 90% to 104%. |
| A05 | Budget values are approved appropriations in nominal naira, not actual spending and not adjusted for inflation. | Budget implementation reports have not yet been reviewed, and no deflator has been sourced. | Budget chart in notebook 01 | Real growth is lower than nominal growth over 2021 to 2026, given high consumer price inflation over the period. |
